Sandwich Sudoku - Brain Puzzle

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

This policy explains how Integrated Media Systems, LLC handles information in connection with Sandwich Sudoku - Brain Puzzle (the “App”), Android package com.sandwichsudokupuzzle.app.

You can play without creating an App account. Game progress and preferences are stored on your device. Advertising and purchases use Google services, which process information as described below.

1. Game progress and device storage

The App saves your current puzzle, entries, notes, undo history, elapsed time, hints, completion status, win statistics, best times, and settings on your device. Settings include your theme, error and line highlighting, completed-number marking, and keep-screen-awake preference. The App also stores a local record of your Remove Ads entitlement.

This information supports saved games, preferences, statistics, and purchase access. The App does not send saved puzzle progress to a developer-operated server and does not offer an App account or developer-operated cloud save service. Depending on your Android backup settings, the operating system may back up or restore App data through your device account.

2. Advertising and consent

The App uses Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial advertisements. Google’s Mobile Ads SDK can collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device and account-related identifiers such as the advertising ID and app set ID, app and advertisement interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. Google uses this information to deliver and measure advertising, provide analytics, and prevent fraud and abuse.

The App requests non-personalized ads. These ads can still involve identifiers, local storage, contextual information, measurement, and fraud-prevention processing; non-personalized does not mean that no information is collected.

The App uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to request and manage advertising consent where applicable. When Google requires a privacy-options entry point, you can reopen it through Settings → Ad privacy choices. You may also manage available advertising controls in your Android device settings.

After a Remove Ads purchase is confirmed, the App stops requesting and displaying banner and interstitial advertisements. This does not delete information already processed by Google.

More information is available in Google’s Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from apps that use its services, and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

3. Google Play purchases

The optional, one-time Remove Ads purchase is processed by Google Play. Google handles your payment details under its own policies. The App does not receive or store your full payment-card information.

The App receives purchase information through Google Play Billing, such as the product identifier, purchase state, acknowledgment state, and purchase token. It uses this information to confirm and acknowledge purchases, grant the ad-free entitlement, and restore access. A local entitlement record is saved on your device. Google retains purchase and transaction records under its policies.

4. Information you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever information, messages, or attachments you choose to provide. We use that information to respond, troubleshoot issues, and handle privacy requests. Please avoid sending passwords, full payment details, or other information unnecessary for your request.

5. How information is used and shared

Information is used to operate the App, remember progress and settings, deliver advertising, manage consent, confirm purchases, prevent fraud, and respond to support requests. Google and its advertising partners process information through their services as described in their policies and applicable consent notices. Our email provider processes correspondence when you contact us.

We may disclose information we hold when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, investigate fraud or abuse, or protect legal rights. Service providers may process information in countries other than the one where you live.

6. Retention and deletion

Saved games, settings, and the local entitlement record remain in App storage until that data is cleared or removed. You can clear local data through Android’s App storage settings or uninstall the App. This erases local progress and settings. Operating-system backups may remain subject to your backup settings and the provider’s retention practices.

Clearing App data does not cancel or refund a Google Play purchase. You can use the App’s purchase-restoration feature with the Google Play account that owns the purchase.

We retain support correspondence as needed to resolve requests and meet applicable legal obligations. Contact us to request deletion of information you sent us. Google’s retention and deletion practices apply to information held by its services; clearing App storage does not delete those records.

7. Your privacy choices and rights

You can manage local App data, available device advertising controls, and advertising consent as described above. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information, object to or restrict certain processing, or withdraw consent. To make a request about information we hold, email us. We may need information to verify and respond to your request. You may also have the right to contact your local data-protection authority.

For information controlled by Google, use the privacy controls and request processes described in Google’s Privacy Policy.

8. Children’s privacy

The App does not ask players to submit a name, date of birth, or contact information to play. Advertising services may process device information as described above. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review the matter and take appropriate action, including deletion where applicable.

9. Security

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information we handle. Device security and your operating-system settings help protect locally stored App data. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App or its information practices change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent.
